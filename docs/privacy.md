# Overwatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overwatch is a tool that checks news articles for writing quality, sourcing, and manipulation patterns.

## What we collect
When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. When you analyze an article, we save your email, the article link, its title, the score, and the analysis, so you can see them in "My history."

## How it is used
- Your email identifies your account and enforces daily usage limits.
- Article text you submit is sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce the analysis.
- Your saved data is stored in Supabase, a database service.

## What we do not do
We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

## Deleting your data
To have your history deleted, email overwatch.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.
